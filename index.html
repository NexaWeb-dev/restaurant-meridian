      Our Story

      A map for people who love a good cup

      Meridian started as a simple idea: the best coffee is rarely where the crowds are. It is in a quiet lane, behind a small window, poured by someone who cares.

      Meridian Coffee Atlas is a concept for a calm, beautiful way to find those places, by city, by neighbourhood, or by what is closest to you right now.

      About this demo. The cafés, phone numbers and opening hours shown here are sample data created for development. They are not real businesses and nothing here is verified. A live version would need a trusted data source and a backend to keep listings and opening hours accurate.
