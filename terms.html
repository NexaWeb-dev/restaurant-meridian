Meridian Coffee Atlas is a design and engineering demonstration. The cafés listed are fictional. Phone numbers use an invalid prefix and cannot reach a real business. Addresses, coordinates and opening hours are illustrative.

No guarantee of accuracy

Distances are straight-line estimates calculated in your browser. Open and closed status is derived from sample schedules and is not live information. Do not rely on anything here to plan a real visit.
